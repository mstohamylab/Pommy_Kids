Returns & Exchanges

Return window: You can return unused items in their original packaging within the period required by Saudi e-commerce regulations, counted from the date of delivery.

Exceptions: For hygiene reasons, underwear and socks cannot be returned once opened, unless faulty.

Refunds: Card, Apple Pay and BNPL orders are refunded to the original payment method; COD orders are refunded by bank transfer.

Business details shown on every page footer: legal name, Commercial Registration (CR) number, VAT number, address, phone and email, plus the Saudi Business Center e-commerce certificate.
Policy wording is a sample. Final texts must be approved by Pommy Kids and its legal advisor.
